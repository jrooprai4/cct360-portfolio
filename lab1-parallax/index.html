My trip to the Calgary Stampede 2025

In June of 2025, I went to the Calgary Stampede with my cousin and her family. I was visiting them while they were there for their holidays.

The night before we went, we decided on a plan on how we were going to get there the next day. We decided on taking the c-train, which is the name of Calgary's streetcar system. Since many people would be taking that route, we had to leave early. Also, more people were likely to go on this day because the event was going to let in anyone who got to the event before 11am for free.

The next morning, we got ready and left their house to catch the c-train. We got our tickets and waited on the platform. My aunt had come up with a plan on how we could beat the traffic on the trains; we had to take one train to a specific point before switching trains. We also knew that other people would likely try doing this too. Once we got to the station where we needed to switch trains, we all got off the train to wait on the platform, which was very crowded.

Once our next train came, we realized the train was full and there was no space for anyone to get on. This ended up happening twice, as the next train also passed by completely full. My aunt then had the idea to take another train and essentially go back by one station and then take the train to the stampede from there, assuming it should be less full. We got on that train and took it to the station; but then we saw that the train we needed to take didn't come to this station.

Once we got to the station, we got off the train and tried looking at the map to see where we were and what trains pass through this station. Time was also running out and we weren’t sure if we would be able to make the 11am cutoff time to get into the stampede for free.

After looking at the map at the station and looking online, we realized that we had basically looped around, by taking a train in the direction we came from, and found another train that would take us to the stampede.

Our new train showed up, thankfully less full, and we took it to the stampede. Since figuring all of this out took quite some time, it had been past 11am.

But, once we got to the stampede and went to get into the event, the security guards let us in for free after checking out stuff! They had still been letting people in for free by the time we showed up.

That was the story of my trip to the Calgary Stampede :)
